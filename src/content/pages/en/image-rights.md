Sport, concert and festival photographs sometimes show people. I try to favour wide shots and to respect everyone, but if you recognise yourself in a picture and would rather it were not published, **just ask me**.

## How to request removal

Email [oceane.saque@gmail.com](mailto:oceane.saque@gmail.com) with:

1. the title of the photo or the link to the page where it appears;
2. how to recognise you in the picture (for example: “the person in the red jacket, on the left”);
3. if you like, what you would prefer: removal of the photo, or cropping / blurring.

You do not need to give a reason or send any ID.

## My commitment

- To acknowledge your request quickly.
- **To remove the photo from the site as soon as possible**, along with its previews (thumbnails, sharing images).
- Not to republish it without your consent.

Search engines may keep a cached copy for a few days after removal; you can also ask them to update it, for example with Google's outdated content removal tool.

## Parents and minors

Requests from a parent or legal guardian about a photo in which a minor can be recognised are handled as a priority.
