## In short

This website sets **no cookies**, uses **no audience measurement tool** and collects **no personal data** when you visit. It loads no third-party services: fonts are hosted on the site itself.

## Data processed

### Messages you send me

If you email me, your address and message are used only to reply to you. They are kept for the duration of our exchange and at most 3 years after our last contact.

### Hosting provider logs

Like any host, **Netlify, Inc.** (United States) may record technical logs (IP address, date, requested page) for security and service operation. This data is processed by Netlify under its [privacy policy](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/), which provides for retention consistent with that purpose or with its legal obligations.

Netlify states that it complies with the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which governs the transfer of this data to the United States.

## Recipients

Your data is never sold, transferred or used for advertising.
