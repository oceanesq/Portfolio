## In short

This website sets **no cookies** and collects **no personal data** by default. It loads no third-party services: fonts are hosted on the site itself. Anonymous audience measurement is only enabled if you accept it.

## Data controller

Océane Saqué — [oceane.saque@gmail.com](mailto:oceane.saque@gmail.com).

## Data processed

### Your audience measurement choice

When you click “Accept” or “Decline”, your choice and its date are stored **in your browser only** (local storage, key `os-consent`), so that you are not asked again on every page. The choice is kept for **6 months**, then asked again. You can change it at any time with the “Audience measurement settings” link at the bottom of every page.
