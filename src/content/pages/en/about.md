My name is Océane. I'm 22 and I split my time between Toulouse and Céret, in the south of France. I work as an engineer, and I've been taking pictures since I was a child: I got my first camera at 10 and have hardly put it down since.

I'm an amateur photographer. This site is a way to share my pictures and to keep improving, with the hope of gradually moving towards semi-professional work.

My photos follow what keeps me going. Nature first, on hikes and travels. Sport, for the way people push their limits and help each other at every race or match. And concerts and festivals, for their colours and their energy.

A question, an idea for a project, or just a word about a picture? Write to me, I'm always happy to reply.
